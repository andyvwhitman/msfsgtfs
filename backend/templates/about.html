About

After missing many ferries by misreading the schedule, and narrowly avoiding many a car accident trying to read it on my phone, I finally decided to build a better ferry schedule. Actually... I've had this idea for nearly four years, and my crippling procrastination habit just never let it see the light of day. Alas...

It's finally here: the better ferry schedule. I've thought meticulously about what's going through my head every time I try to catch the ferry:

  • When is the next ferry?
  • Will I make it?
  • When is the ferry after that?
  • How much lollygagging time do I have?
  • When is the last ferry?

I built the tool I need to manage island life, even though I've since departed :(

I feel like this goes without saying, but the world always shocks you:

Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Maine State Ferry Service. Schedules are accurate to the best of our ability, but always confirm with the official source before you travel — if you miss the boat, you're on your own.

If you have any comments or ideas that can make this a better tool for you, feel free to reach out and let me know: andyvwhitman@gmail.com.

If you don't like it, missed the ferry because of it, or just feel like you have to be heard, feel free to not reach out I guess? It's a free country... do what you want... I won't stop you...

Happy sailing!

— Andy & Karter
